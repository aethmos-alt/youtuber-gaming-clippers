# script.md: own video #1, "the full GTA 6 map"

Format: **ranking + guide**. Every region of Leonida, ranked worst to best, shot from your own
gameplay only. The #1 region is shown first as the payoff.

Length target: **10-12 min.** The two map outliers from step 2 ran 9 min
(ABTW, "The Entire GTA 6 Map Explained", 33.6x its channel's median) and 14 min (BreeZe GTA,
"The GTA 6 Map Makes No Sense", 33x). Map/explainer outliers overall cluster at 9-15 min.

Fact rule: region names below come from Rockstar's pre-launch material and also appear on the
outlier thumbnails. Everything marked **[VERIFY]** is something you must check in your own
game before recording the voiceover. I have not played GTA 6 and will not invent details.

---

## 0:00-0:20 Cold open (the payoff first)

- **Record:** the single best-looking shot in the game. A slow helicopter or plane pass
  over your #1 region at golden hour, 15-20 s, no HUD (photo/cinematic mode if available [VERIFY]).
- **VO:** "This is the best place in GTA 6, and by the end you'll know exactly why. But first,
  the worst one, because I ranked every single region of Leonida."
- Cut straight to the worst region on "worst one". No logo, no "hey guys".

## 0:20-0:50 The whole map in one shot

- **Record:** the pause-menu map, fully zoomed out, slowly zooming to each region as you name it.
  Then a 10 s flight timelapse across the full length of the state.
- **Record:** the drive-time test. Drive from one end of the map to the other without stopping
  and screen-record the timer. Thumbnails in step 3 used a "40 MIN DRIVE" route line;
  get your own real number [VERIFY].
- **VO:** "<N> regions, <your measured drive time> end to end. Here's every one, ranked."

## 0:50-9:30 The ranking (about 60-75 s per region)

Rank the regions after you've played them. Don't decide the order from this script. For
**each** region, record the same five shots so every segment cuts the same way:

1. **Establishing:** an aerial pass, 8-10 s.
2. **Street level:** drive through the main road, 15 s.
3. **Best secret or detail:** one thing most players miss (an easter egg, a hidden spot, a
   unique activity) [VERIFY each].
4. **Something to do:** a mission start, an activity, a shop, or a vehicle you can only get there [VERIFY].
5. **The verdict shot:** stop, a character on screen, with the rank number added in the edit.

Regions to cover (names from Rockstar pre-launch material; confirm the full list in-game [VERIFY]):
- Vice City
- Leonida Keys

- Ambrosia

## 9:30-10:30 #1 and the reveal
